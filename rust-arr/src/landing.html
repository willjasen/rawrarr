Privacy, clearly

Designed with boundaries.

Rawrarr separates account sign-in and device coordination from the nodes that work with your libraries and media.

  • ✓Media files remain on storage you control.
  • ✓Shared configuration and metadata are designed to be age-encrypted before synchronization.
  • ✓Your age private identity is not needed by the coordinator.
  • ✓The coordinator can still see account, device, routing, timing, and traffic-size information.
Early project: Rawrarr is under active development. Some content flows are incomplete or not yet verified end to end, and the project has not had an independent security audit. These design goals should not be read as a claim that every current flow already meets them.
