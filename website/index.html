Your library.
Your devices.

rawrarr brings your Radarr and Sonarr libraries, devices, and storage into one self-hosted media workspace.

Your devices connect through a coordinator to share a view of your setup. Media files and private keys stay on your devices; shared configuration and metadata are designed for age-encrypted synchronization.

Open app

Still in development. Encryption flows are not all verified end to end, and rawrarr has not had an independent security audit.

Privacy & analytics

The coordinator can see account, device, routing, timing, and traffic-size information. rawrarr is a media workspace, not a video player or a download client.

Anonymous site analytics. Self-hosted Umami receives a fixed page label, not page URLs, query strings, referrers, or user actions. It processes visitor IP addresses and browser user agents to calculate anonymous visit metrics.
