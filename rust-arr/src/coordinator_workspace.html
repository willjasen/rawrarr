    About rawrarr

    A shared library for your devices

    rawrarr connects the media tools you already use with a private, synchronized library of useful metadata.

    Radarr and Sonarr manage your movie and TV libraries. Connect them in Settings to browse library data in rawrarr. Devices with access to your media can share age-encrypted metadata and artwork so the same information is available across your devices.

    Your media files stay on the storage you choose. Roarlay coordinates your account and devices; fossils connect to your media tools and share encrypted library metadata.

    Rawrarr currently focuses on media libraries and their metadata. General-purpose sharing of files and folders is a future direction.
