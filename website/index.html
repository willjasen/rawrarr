Privacy, clearly

Useful boundaries.
Honest limits.

Rawrarr is built around keeping your media and private keys under your control. Here is what that means today.

01

Your media stays on your storage. The coordinator handles device coordination; media files remain on storage you control.

02

Private age identities stay with you. The coordinator does not need your age private identity.

03

Coordination still reveals metadata. The coordinator can see account, device, routing, timing, and traffic-size information.

04

Encryption support is still developing. Shared configuration and metadata are designed to be age-encrypted before synchronization; some content flows are incomplete or not yet verified end to end.

r

A project in progress

Early, evolving, and still in development.

Rawrarr is under active development. Features and workflows are still changing, and the project has not had an independent security audit. Treat its privacy and security goals as direction, not a certification of every current flow.

Rawrarr is a media workspace, not a video player or a download client.
