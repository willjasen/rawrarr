BUILT FOR YOUR DEVICES

What rawrarr does

rawrarr brings your media libraries together across devices. Radarr and Sonarr provide the library views, while rawrarr organizes shared metadata and artwork so your devices can use the same information.

Metadata shared between nodes is age-encrypted before it is synchronized. Roarlay helps your signed-in account manage its devices, and boneyards keep media on their own disks.

Use Movies and TV to connect Radarr and Sonarr and browse their libraries. Settings contains account and node configuration.

How the pieces fit

rawrarr coordinates your library and keeps shared metadata available across your devices.

Radarr and Sonarr manage movie and TV libraries. Connect each service on its Movies or TV page to see its library data in rawrarr.

Your devices synchronize encrypted shared metadata. Media files remain on the storage you choose; rawrarr does not upload them to the coordinator.

rawrarr is evolving. General-purpose sharing of files and folders is a future direction; the current app focuses on media libraries and their metadata.
